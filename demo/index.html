The benchmark

A multilingual view of transcript ambiguity

Each item is a pair of valid written representations of the same spoken utterance. Filters below update the distribution and open a matching example in the output explorer.

Five-category taxonomy

From enumerable forms to language-specific ambiguity

Numeric expressions and symbols are language-agnostic at the phenomenon level. Abbreviations, orthographic variants, and homophonic substitutions depend on each language.
